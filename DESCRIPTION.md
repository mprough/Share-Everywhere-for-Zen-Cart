# Share Everywhere for Zen Cart

Give shoppers an easy way to share products from your Zen Cart store. Share Everywhere adds a configurable widget to product pages with 95 built-in actions for social sharing, messaging, bookmarking, store profiles, and device tools.

- Choose five layouts independently for desktop and mobile. Expand the compact or single-button layout on click or mouseover.
- Enable, order, and pin individual buttons. Choose three button sizes, show or hide the share title, and use monochrome, platform, or custom icon colors.
- Offer Facebook, Pinterest, X, Reddit, Threads, WhatsApp, Telegram, email, SMS, and more. Add store links for platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Discord.
- Add and preview custom share links or store profile links. Offer Native Share where supported, Copy link, Print, and a locally generated product QR code.
- Manage everything under Catalog > Share Everywhere in Zen Cart admin.

Free download for Zen Cart 1.5.8, 2.0.x, 2.1.x, 2.2.x, and 3.0.0. Installs through Plugin Manager. Installation Extra.
